## Full description

QR Snap is a QR code scanner and barcode scanner for Android. Scan a QR code with the camera or from a gallery image, create QR codes and barcodes, check a link before you open it, export and back up your history, share results, and run a small Point of Sale on the device.

**Scan QR codes and barcodes**
Use live camera scan for QR codes and many barcode formats. Scan a QR code from a photo in your gallery. Camera batch scan is free. Batch scan from gallery images is included with Lifetime Unlock.

**QR code generator**
Create 14+ types: URL, text, email, phone, SMS, Wi-Fi, contact/vCard, calendar event, location, social profiles, and product barcodes (EAN, ISBN, and related formats as supported in-app). Preview, add an icon, save to history and favorites.

**Link safety and email checks**
Scanning only reads data. Opening a link can be risky. QR Snap runs a URL safety check (Google Web Risk plus pattern checks) and shows a clear safe, risk, or unavailable result before you open the link. Everyone gets 5 URL checks per day. Email domain (MX) validation is unlimited and does not use URL credits.

**History on your device**
Scan and create history, favorites, folders, and smart filters. Storage is local. No account. No cloud sync of your codes.

**Export, backup, restore, share (Lifetime Unlock)**
Export as CSV, JSON, image ZIP, and parsed text as shown in-app. Local backup and restore. Share to other apps on the phone.

**Point of Sale (Lifetime Unlock)**
Keep a local product list, scan to cart, review sales history and reports, and generate PDF receipts or set up a thermal printer where the platform allows. POS data stays on the device.

**Pricing**
Free: scan, generate, history, ads, 5 URL checks per day, unlimited email checks. Watch a rewarded ad for +2 URL checks (max 2 per 24 hours, 15-minute cooldown).

Lifetime Unlock ($9.99 one-time): ads removed, gallery batch scan, backup, restore, export, share, Point of Sale. Still 5 URL checks per day.

URL packs: +30 ($0.99), +100 ($2.99), +250 ($4.99). Credits never expire on that install. They cannot be restored after uninstall, app-data clear, or a new device. Lifetime Unlock restores with the same Google Play account.

Failed or inconclusive URL checks do not consume credits. Play Store prices in your region are the prices that apply.

QR Snap is built with Flutter. Scan, generate, history, and POS work offline. Link safety checks need internet.
