# Pricing — QR Snap

QR Snap pricing is Free forever for scan and generate, plus an optional one-time Lifetime Unlock and optional URL safety check packs. Localized Google Play and App Store prices are authoritative.

## Free
- Price: $0 forever
- Limits: 5 URL safety checks per day; unlimited email domain (MX) checks
- Features: live camera scan, scan from gallery, camera batch scan, generate 14+ QR and barcode types, history, favorites, folders, search, result actions (open, call, SMS, email, save contact, add event, maps, copy)
- Ads: banner and occasional interstitial
- Bonus: watch a rewarded ad for +2 URL checks (max 2 ads per rolling 24 hours, 15-minute cooldown)
- Not included: gallery batch scan, backup/restore, export, share flows, Point of Sale

## Lifetime Unlock
- Price: $9.99 one-time (not a subscription)
- SKU: qrsnap_lifetime_unlock
- Limits: same 5 URL checks per day as Free
- Features: everything in Free, plus:
  - Ads removed permanently
  - Batch scan from gallery images
  - Local backup and restore
  - Export: CSV, JSON, image ZIP, parsed text as shown in-app
  - Share to other apps
  - Point of Sale (inventory, scan-to-cart, sales history, receipts)
- Restores: yes, with the same Google Play / App Store account (Restore on the Plans screen)

## Point of Sale (included in Lifetime Unlock)
- Local shop / products inventory
- Scan-to-cart checkout
- Sales history and reports
- Receipt PDF / thermal printer setup where the platform allows
- Data stays on the device; backup with Lifetime tools
- User is responsible for tax and receipt legality in their jurisdiction

## Starter Pack
- Price: $0.99
- SKU: qrsnap_url_credits_30
- Credits: +30 URL safety checks
- Expiry: never on this install
- Restores: no. Tied to the anonymous installation wallet. Lost on uninstall, app-data clear, or a new device

## Standard Pack

- Expiry: never on this install
- Restores: no

## Value Pack

- SKU: qrsnap_url_credits_250

- Expiry: never on this install
- Restores: no

## Credit consumption order
1. Free daily checks (5/day)
2. Rewarded-ad bonus checks
3. Purchased credit packs

## Notes
- Failed or inconclusive URL checks do not consume credits
- Email domain checks are always free and unlimited
- Purchased URL credits cannot be transferred or restored after uninstall, app-data clear, or a new device
- Lifetime Unlock restores with the same store account
- Older monthly subscription SKUs may still be honored until natural expiry; new purchases use Lifetime + packs only
- Prices shown are USD fallback; store prices in the app are authoritative
